No hype. No fake numbers. Every entry shows its receipts or is marked unverified.

## Honest caveats

- **$0 documented revenue.** Three experiments are live, five haven't started or are still research. We publish the zeros.
- **$1M is a hypothesis, not a forecast.** This registry exists to find out what's real.
- **Failed experiments are welcome.** A documented failure ("tried X, lost $40, doesn't work because Y") is some of the most valuable data here — it saves everyone else the $40.
